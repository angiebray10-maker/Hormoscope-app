HORMOscope app map

Science, not stars. A cycle app with Attuned Partner Mode for couples.

Main tabs

  • Home: your cycle day, phase, quick log and daily read.
  • Journal: private daily journal, one day per page.
  • Attunement: invite your partner or sync cycles with your girlfriend.
  • Calendar: periods, fertile window, symptoms and intimacy.
  • Profile: your details, partner visibility and settings.

Partner

  • Partner Hub: where a partner enters an invite code.
