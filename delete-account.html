HORMOscope Account Deletion

HORMOscope is developed and operated by Angelita Braaten. This page lets you permanently delete your HORMOscope account and its associated personal information, without installing the app. Questions about deletion: hormoscope@gmail.com.

Two ways to delete your account

  • In the app: open HORMOscope, go to Profile, and tap "Delete My Account" under Sign Out.
  • On this page: use the secure form below. It signs you in the same way the app does, asks you to confirm, and then deletes your account right away in the same session.

Delete your account here

Enter the email and password of the account you want to delete. Your password is sent only to the HORMOscope server over an encrypted connection, exactly as when you sign in to the app. It is never shown publicly and never stored by this page.

Are you sure?

This permanently deletes the account for and everything in it:

  • Your cycle history and daily logs
  • Your journal, including sealed vault days
  • Your intimacy records
  • Your AI conversations and journal companion
  • Your partner connections (they are disconnected)

This cannot be undone, and the email will no longer sign in.

Your account is deleted

Your account and your personal data have been removed. That email can no longer sign in to HORMOscope. Thank you for the time you spent here, and take good care of yourself.

If you cannot sign in

Email hormoscope@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will reply to that same address to confirm the request really came from you before we delete anything. Never send your password by email. We will never ask for it. Only a request confirmed from the account's own email address will be processed, so nobody can delete another person's account this way.

What is deleted, and what is retained

When a deletion is confirmed, your account and personal data are deleted right away: your profile, cycle logs, journal entries, intimacy records, AI conversations, partner connections, notification settings, and account sign-in. There is no waiting period and no copy kept for you to come back to, so save anything you want before deleting.

One narrow exception, for legal and accounting reasons: our own payment transaction logs are stripped of your name, email, and account link at the moment of deletion, and the remaining anonymous amounts and dates are kept as business accounting records. They no longer identify you. Records held by payment processors such as Google Play, Stripe, and RevenueCat are kept under those companies' own policies and are not ours to erase.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. If you have an active HORMOscope Pro subscription through Google Play, deleting your account does not stop Google Play billing. Cancel it in the Play Store under Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
