Privacy Policy

Security & privacy
  • Your data travels over HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
  • Passwords are stored only as secure hashes (bcrypt), never as plain text.
  • We never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from Profile.
  • We follow Google Play's User Data and health app policies.

Your Privacy Matters

At HORMOscope, we understand the sensitive nature of the information you share with us. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your data is handled with the utmost care and security.

Information We Collect

  • Account information (email, name)
  • Cycle tracking data (period dates, cycle length)
  • Health-related notes and symptoms you choose to log
  • Journal entries and notes you choose to log
  • Intimacy tracking data (premium users only)

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide personalized cycle insights and predictions
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • To send you relevant notifications (with your consent)

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your information is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We never sell your personal data. When you use the Journal Companion or AI features, the text you write and your cycle context are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) only to generate a reply.

Your Rights

  • Access your personal data at any time
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your cycle tracking history
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection

You can delete your account and its data yourself at any time, in the app under Profile, or on our account deletion page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@hormoscope.app
